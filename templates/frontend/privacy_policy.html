1. Introduction & Overview

At PyLoom Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("PyLoom", "we", "our", or "us"), we are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information when you access our website (https://pyloomtech.com), engage our enterprise Data Science and Data Engineering offshore services, enroll in PyLoom Academy training programs, or interact with our software applications, including applications integrated with Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, and Graph API).

By using our website, applications, or services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and enhance our services. The types of information we collect include:

  • Contact and Identity Information: Name, professional title, company name, email address, phone number, and physical mailing address provided via our contact forms, service inquiry forms, or training applications.
  • Service & Engagement Data: Project specifications, data pipeline requirements, business goals, and communication history exchanged when scoping or delivering enterprise data solutions.
  • Training & Academy Information: Student registration details, course enrollments, attendance, and feedback submitted during certification programs.
  • Automatically Collected Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, time spent on pages, device identifiers, and server access logs.

4. Meta / Facebook Platform Data

When you interact with PyLoom Technologies applications integrated with Meta (including Facebook Login, Instagram Graph API, or Meta business tools):

  • Authorized Permissions Only: We only request and access the specific user profile information (such as basic public profile, name, and verified email address) that you explicitly authorize through the standard Meta permissions dialog.
  • No Password Storage: We never receive, process, or store your Facebook or Meta credentials. Authentication occurs securely through Meta's OAuth 2.0 protocol.
  • Purpose Limitation: Any data obtained via Meta platform tools is used strictly to identify your account, personalize your service experience, or verify authorization to access requested services. We never sell, broker, or transfer Meta user data to advertising networks or data brokers.
  • Compliance with Meta Platform Terms: We strictly adhere to the Meta Developer Platform Terms and developer policies.

5. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data for legitimate business purposes, including:

Delivering offshore data engineering pods, analytics engineering, cloud lakehouse architectures, and training certifications.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose personal information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist with website hosting, cloud server infrastructure, database backups, and analytics, bound by contractual data processing and confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal & Regulatory Obligations: When disclosure is required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of PyLoom Technologies, our clients, or the public.
  • Corporate Restructuring: In connection with or during negotiations of any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, satisfy our contractual commitments, resolve disputes, and comply with statutory legal and accounting retention obligations. Once data is no longer required, it is securely anonymized or permanently deleted from our servers.

8. Cookies & Analytics Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to ensure functionality, remember user preferences, and gather aggregated visitor statistics via Google Analytics.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive features on our website.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws (including GDPR and global privacy frameworks), you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request that we permanently delete your personal information from our databases.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Object to or restrict the processing of your data under specific conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request that your data be provided in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection team at info@pyloomtech.com.
