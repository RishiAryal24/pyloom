Data Privacy & Control

User Data Deletion Instructions

How to remove PyLoom Technologies applications and request complete deletion of your data.

In compliance with Meta Platform Terms & General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR)

Your Right to Delete Your Data

PyLoom Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("PyLoom") respects your privacy and control over your personal data. If you have connected your Meta (Facebook) account with our application or submitted personal data through our website, you have the right to revoke access and request the permanent deletion of your data from our systems.

Follow These Steps to Delete Your Data

1

Step 1: Remove PyLoom from Your Facebook Account

To immediately disconnect our application from your Facebook profile and stop any future data synchronization:

  1. Open Facebook and log into your account on desktop or via the mobile application.
  2. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner and select Settings & Privacy, then click Settings.
  3. In the left-hand navigation menu under Permissions, click on Apps and Websites (or go directly to https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications).
  4. Scroll through your list of active applications to find PyLoom Technologies (or the related PyLoom application).
  5. Click the Remove button next to the application.
  6. In the pop-up confirmation window, check the box if you wish to delete all posts, videos, or events that the app may have published on your behalf, and click Remove to confirm.
Outcome: This immediately revokes PyLoom's token and prevents the application from making any future Graph API calls or accessing your Facebook profile.
2

Step 2: Request Complete Server Data Deletion from PyLoom

If you also wish to permanently purge all existing records, contact submissions, training registrations, and account identifiers stored on our secure servers:

Option A: Send Email Request

Send an email to our Data Protection Team at:

info@pyloomtech.com

Subject Line: User Data Deletion Request

Option B: Online Form

Submit your deletion request directly via our contact form:

Open Contact Form

Select "Data Privacy / Deletion" in your inquiry.

Please include the following details in your request:
  • Full Name: Your registered name or Facebook profile name.
  • Email Address: The primary email associated with your Facebook account or PyLoom submissions.
  • Phone Number: (Optional) If provided during event/training registration.
  • Scope: Specify whether you want all data deleted or a specific training/inquiry record purged.

What Happens Next? (Processing Timeline)

Acknowledgment within 48 Hours Our Data Protection Team will verify receipt of your deletion request and confirm identity matching to prevent unauthorized tampering.
Server Data Purge within 30 Days We permanently remove your personal identifiable information (PII), database records, interaction history, and OAuth tokens from our production databases and backup archives within thirty (30) business days.
Final Completion Notice You will receive a final confirmation email confirming that all associated data has been permanently and irreversibly expunged.
Legal & Regulatory Exceptions: Certain aggregated or anonymized analytical records that do not identify you, or records that we are legally required to retain under applicable Nepali commercial tax, accounting, or audit laws, may be retained in encrypted archives for the minimum statutory period.
